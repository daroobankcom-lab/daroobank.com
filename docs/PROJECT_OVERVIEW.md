# Project overview

**Status:** vision and proposed scope, not delivered functionality. DarooBank (داروبانک) is a Persian-first knowledge and intelligence service for patients, pharmacists, physicians and pharmaceutical researchers in Iran. It is not a medicine seller or a claim of real-time pharmacy stock.

## Outcomes

- Find an identifiable drug concept and navigate to a clinically reviewed patient or professional monograph with evidence and review date.
- Distinguish each local product and package and display only sourced, reviewed prices with currency, category, unit and freshness.
- Operate independent, authorized data collection with auditable decisions and no publication on partial or conflicting data.
- Preserve modular boundaries for future institutional APIs, mobile clients and regulated consultation.

## Users and guardrails

Patients need understandable information and escalation instructions; professionals need detailed evidence and limitations; editors and reviewers need accountable publication workflows; data stewards need product-level validation. Sponsored information must be identifiable and cannot influence clinical approval. A drug record, brand, formulation and package are not equivalent. Absence of evidence is never proof of safety or availability.

## Early non-goals

Online sales, autonomous advice/diagnosis, unverified interaction conclusions, pharmacy stock claims, scraped proprietary text and live consultations. Any future medical or price release is gated on verified sources, staffing, licensing and safety review. See [requirements](PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md) and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
